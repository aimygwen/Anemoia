The Backstory

My Roots

Growing up in a quiet countryside by a lake, I was moved by the same golden sunset every evening — a warm, nostalgic hug that never left my heart.

PS1 games, Game Boys, and retro tech led to pixels placed one by one for fun. Creating became second nature, from traditional drawings to digital canvases.

Coming Back

Somewhere along the way, that feeling faded. Rediscovering old pixelated textures and edgy lowpoly models gave both my life and my art a new meaning.

There is beauty in minimalism broken to its core — in transitions, change, and the quiet spaces between moments.

Every piece of art is a compilation of spent hours, fixations, and core memories — the things we love become passive stats that shape how we build and why we make.

Inspirations & Mindset

Artistic Influence

My biggest inspiration is Violet Schofield — discovering her lowpoly work gave me a clear vision for what I wanted to build.

When I feel at home

Quiet transitional moments — a long train ride with headphones, or lost places with unknown history. I've always felt more at home in the dark than in daylight.

Why?

The sea keeps me motivated: waves, horizon, sea breeze. Building toward that peace is what keeps me moving forward every day.
